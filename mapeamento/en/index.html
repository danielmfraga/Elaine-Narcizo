The workshop

Discover the size of your world of relationships.

  • 2 hlong
  • Onlinefrom wherever you are
  • up to 10people per group
  • R$ 50per participant

The goal is to broaden your own concept of relationship, recognizing that you already take part in a relational world far wider than you usually assume. It is not about valuing your relationships more, but about discovering the extent and diversity of the relationships that already make up your life.

What can this produce?

  • Recognizing that your world already offers resources, supports and possibilities that often go unnoticed. This discovery can ease the feeling that everything depends on you, reduce the demands you place on yourself and make life lighter.
  • Moving out of isolation and loneliness by recognizing the community of life you already belong to, even if it is very different from the one you imagined or recognized until now.

How will it be done?

We will work with Relationship Mapping, an instrument that Elaine developed over 20 years of practice as an educator and social worker. To take part, all you need is a sheet of paper, colored pencils and a pen.

You will be able to reproduce this instrument in your own work with people and groups.

It is a reflective group experience, based on questions. It does not replace psychological care.
